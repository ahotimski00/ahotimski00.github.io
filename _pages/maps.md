## Timberland Ownership, West Virginia

Maps produced from the Land Ownership Change Detection project showing parent LLC ownership patterns across WV counties.

*See the [Projects page](/projects/#land-ownership) for full project context and the interactive image carousel.*

## Urban Growth in Fire Hazard Severity Zones, LA County

Land cover classification maps showing urban expansion within CAL FIRE fire hazard severity zones from 2007 to 2025.

*Classified using Random Forest on Landsat imagery in Google Earth Engine.*

---

## Value Density, Lehigh County PA

Choropleth maps comparing total and per-acre taxable assessment values across residential, warehouse, and mixed-use parcels.

---

## Urban Heat Vulnerability, Boston Housing Authority

Interactive ArcGIS Online map developed during research with the Boston Housing Authority identifying heat exposure risk at BHA properties across Boston.
